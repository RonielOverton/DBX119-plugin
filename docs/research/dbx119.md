# dbx 119 — research notes

What the plugin is modeled on, with the source for each fact. Items marked **unverified** need measurements from the owner's unit (the owner has a working dbx 119; see `tools/calibration/`).

Source quality: **[M]** = original dbx 117/119 instruction manual (scan supplied by the owner, 2026-11-17; page numbers are the manual's own), **[F]** = forum post by a technician or owner, **[S]** = seller or review site.

The manual is not stored in this repository (copyright). The owner has the PDF ("dBx 117-119 Manual.pdf", 12 pages, from [groupdiy](https://groupdiy.com/threads/dbx-119.14522/)).

## Summary: what the manual settles

| Question | Answer | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Topology | **Feed-forward.** The RMS detector (M3) is fed from the two inputs, before the VCAs. | [M] schematic p.12 |
| Stereo linking | **Linked.** One detector for both channels. The two inputs are summed through R5 and R15 (100 kΩ each) into the detector, so it measures the RMS of **L + R** (the signals, not their powers). | [M] schematic p.12; p.9 item 4 |
| Detector low-frequency roll-off | C11 (0.1 µF) and R4 (1 kΩ) in series into the detector. With R5‖R15 = 50 kΩ this is a high-pass at roughly **31 Hz**. dbx suggests 0.033 µF or 0.01 µF to make it less sensitive to rumble. | [M] p.12, p.13 |
| Gain law | VCA gain changes 3 mV/dB at the R40/R41 junction (6 mV/dB at OA4's output, since R40 = R41 = 220 Ω). Detector output is -6 mV per dB. | [M] p.10 |
| Compress/Expand control | OA4 is the control amplifier: gain -1 at full EXPAND, +1/3 at full COMPRESS (117), set by the 20 kΩ EXP-COMP pot R37 with R35 (1 kΩ) and R36 (2.2 kΩ). -1 gives slope 2.0; +1/3 gives slope 0.67 (spec says 0.7). | [M] p.10, p.12 |
| Infinite compression (119) | Shorting R35 extends compression to infinity. The 119 supplement says its compression range is extended to ∞:1. | [M] p.13, 119 supplement |
| Unity-gain point | LEVEL MATCH (117) / THRESHOLD (119), pot R31 (20 kΩ). 117 range: 100 mV to 1 V (-20 to 0 dBV). 119: range "increased about double", calibrated every 10 dB. | [M] p.15, supplement |
| Above Threshold mode | Only the part of the signal whose RMS level is above the THRESHOLD setting is expanded or compressed, at the slope set by the main control. | [M] supplement |
| Threshold LED | Lights whenever the program level exceeds the threshold. **It does not light in LINEAR mode.** | [M] supplement |
| Release | Constant rate: 140 dB/s (NORM), 50 dB/s (SLOW), measured at 1.4 expansion. The 119 has no SLOW position. | [M] p.15, supplement |
| Attack | 50 dB/ms maximum initial attack rate at 1.4 expansion. **12 ms to 65 %** of the final value at any setting. (The earlier OCR text said 85 %; the scan says 65 %.) | [M] p.15 |
| Low-level limit | R33, D5 and R34 limit the maximum gain increase at low levels, to avoid oscillation through cable capacitance. Removing D5 and shorting R33 removes the limit. | [M] p.10, p.13 |
| Release components | C12 (27 µF, 5 %) is the RMS averaging capacitor. C13 (27 µF, 5 %) keeps the response time matched to the Compress/Expand setting. C14 (100 µF) with R42 (3.3 kΩ) is the 117 SLOW release. Removing C13 speeds up release; raising C14 and lowering R42 slows it. | [M] p.10, p.13 |

## Circuit description [M]

- **Input and VCA:** each channel enters through C7/C17 (0.22 µF) and R6/R16 (100 kΩ) into the VCA (M1, M2), a current-in, current-out device with a virtual-ground input (±20 mV offset). A 741 op-amp (OA1, OA2) with 100 kΩ feedback (R12, R22) and 33 pF (C8, C18) converts the output current back to voltage. Gain is near unity with zero control voltage. Output through 470 Ω (R13, R23). (p.10, p.12)
- **VCA symmetry:** trimmers R8 and R18 are set at the factory for minimum even-order harmonic distortion (adjusted with a 100 Hz, 1 V tone, watching the 200 Hz output). (p.10, p.11)
- **RMS module (M3):** virtual-ground input. Output at the junction of R28 and OA3, -6 mV per dB. R27 balances negative and positive signal sensing (adjusted with the control at 2.0 for minimum 200 Hz output). OA3 is a unity-gain buffer. (p.10, p.11)
- **Control amplifier (OA4):** sums the detector output, the LEVEL MATCH/THRESHOLD offset (R31 network) and drives both VCAs through R40/R41. (p.10, p.12)
- **Power:** unregulated ±15 V for the op-amps; Q1–Q4 regulate +7 V and Q5/Q6 regulate -10 V for the modules. (p.10)

## Published specifications (117) [M] p.15

| Spec | Value |
|---|---|
| Compression & expansion | From 1.4 compression slope (14 dB out per 20 dB in) to 2.0 expansion slope (20 dB out per 10 dB in). 119: compression to ∞:1. |
| Dynamic range | 120 dB input or output |
| Input noise | -110 dBm typical, -100 dBm maximum |
| Input level | over 30 V RMS |
| Input impedance | 50 kΩ |
| Output | 10 V RMS open circuit; +17 dBm into 600 Ω; output impedance 500 Ω |
| Distortion | 0.5 % max at 20 Hz; 0.2 % typical above 50 Hz for complete cycle of compression and expansion; 1.2 % at 20 Hz for 2:1 expansion; 0.05 % typical at 1 kHz and above, any setting (3 V RMS or less) |
| Frequency response | +0, -1 dB, 20 Hz–20 kHz |
| Attack | 50 dB/ms max initial rate at 1.4 expansion; 12 ms to 65 % of final value at any setting |

## Open questions (for the owner's measurements)

1. **Release rate:** whether "140 dB/s at 1.4 expansion" is the output level, the gain, or the detector rate. Output change at slope 1.4 implies a detector rate of 100 dB/s; gain change implies 350 dB/s. The attack/release test decides.
2. **Attack shape:** "65 % of final value" — in dB or in linear terms. The plugin's current detector reaches 85 % (in dB) in about 5 ms.
3. **Knob law:** how the printed knob values map to the slope on the 119 (R37 with R35 shorted). Needs the front-panel markings and the static-curve test.
4. **Threshold range:** where "about double" the 117's 100 mV–1 V lands, and the panel markings.
5. **Low-level limit:** how far the gain can rise at low levels (R33/D5/R34) and what limits the gain range. A forum trace suggests about ±20 dB; the static-curve test at full compression shows it.
6. **The 119's extra parts:** a forum trace mentions a threshold diode and an extra op-amp (OA5) driving the LED. Board photos would confirm how Above Threshold is built.
