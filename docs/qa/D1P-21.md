## Manual steps for the owner

Load `build/RTO119_artefacts/Release/VST3/RTO 119 Compander.vst3` (or the Standalone app) after `cmake --build build --config Release`. Unless a step says otherwise, use a 48 kHz session, AMOUNT at 0, OUTPUT at 0 dB, STEREO LINK on, MODE Linear. Iron is off at first load. Turn it on by clicking the Iron toggle.

1. **Bass, low level (row 28).** Put a bass line or sub tone on a stereo track at a low level (about -30 dBFS peak). Toggle Iron off and on. Expect little change at this level.
2. **Bass, higher level (row 28).** Raise the bass to about -6 dBFS peak. Toggle Iron off and on. Expect a thicker low end, more than at step 1. Listen for level change too: with Iron on the low end measured about 2.2 dB quieter (see Findings), so match levels by ear before judging "thicker".
3. **Vocal and hi-hat at normal level (row 29).** Play a vocal or hi-hat loop at normal mix level. Toggle Iron. Expect almost no difference.
4. **Click test (row 30).** Play a sustained bass note and a kick loop. Toggle Iron on and off 10 times while playing. Expect no click or pop at any toggle. Repeat with a fast automation sweep of the toggle if the host allows.
5. **Session save and reload (row 31).** Set Iron on. Save the session, close it, and reopen it. Iron should still be on. Repeat with Iron off. Check that the toggle state matches what you set.
6. **CPU (row 32).** Watch the host CPU meter with Iron off, then on, on a single instance with a loud bass track. Note the difference. Expect it to be acceptable for your use.
7. **Tooltip (row 33).** Hover over the Iron toggle for about one second. Expect the text "Optional output-transformer color. Not part of the original unit. Off by default."
8. **Extremes (row 34).** With Iron on, set AMOUNT to full compression and then full expansion, and THRESHOLD to its lowest and highest settings. Play a bass and a drum loop. Expect no instability, no clicks, and no crackle that changes with the setting.
9. **Mono and stereo (row 35).** Insert one instance on a mono track and one on a stereo track. Toggle Iron on each. Expect the same behavior: thicker low end on bass, no change on hi-hat.
10. **Sample rates (row 36).** Repeat steps 1 to 4 in a 44.1 kHz session and a 96 kHz session. Expect the same behavior at each rate.

Known limitations (accepted by the operator; not failures):
- The 4x IIR oversampler adds about 3.4 samples of delay. It is not reported to the host (`getLatencyInSamples()` stays 0). During the 20 ms crossfade, dry and wet partly cancel at high frequencies. Switching Iron moves timing by about 3.4 samples.
- The stage is odd-symmetric, so the harmonics are odd (mainly 3rd). The "3rd over 2nd" test (row 9) passes trivially: the 2nd harmonic is at the noise floor, and the ratio is 270 dB. It shows odd-order distortion, not the 3rd-over-2nd balance. Treat it as weak.
- The drive constants (`kDrive`, `kAmount`, 120 Hz and 40 kHz corners) are assumptions tuned by ear, not measured against a transformer. The source comment says so.

Other gaps and notes (none fail the story):
- Low: at 50 Hz and -6 dBFS the stage lowers the level by about 2.2 dB (ad hoc run, 44.1, 48 and 96 kHz host). Peak at 50 Hz full scale is -2.98 dBFS. This is not in the acceptance criteria. It is a plausible result of saturating the low band, but it means "thicker" can be heard partly as "quieter". Owner step 2 asks for level matching.
- Low: the "very little at low level" check at 50 Hz (row 8) is relative only. It compares loud and quiet levels. There is no absolute limit at 50 Hz.
- Low: the no-DC test (row 12) uses one signal only, 50 Hz at -6 dBFS. The stage is odd-symmetric, so DC is expected to be zero for any input, but the suite does not show this for other inputs.
- Low: the Iron tests run only at 192 kHz (4x 48 kHz). The 44.1 and 96 kHz host rates were checked ad hoc (row 21), not in the suite. The suite could take a rate parameter.
- Note: bypass skips Iron (row 27), because `processBlock` returns before `processIron`. This looks right, but the owner should confirm it is the intended behavior. The Iron filter state is not updated during bypass. Expect no audible effect beyond a short settle after bypass ends.
- Note: the no-allocation claim for the JUCE oversampler (row 15) relies on JUCE's documented behavior. JUCE is not in this tree, so this was not checked against the source. The owner's CI build, or a check against the JUCE sources, would confirm it.
- Note: the plugin build (`RTO119_BUILD_PLUGIN=ON`) was not run by QA in this session. The operator reports a clean VST3 and Standalone build. Confirm in CI (`.github/workflows/build.yml`) before merging.
